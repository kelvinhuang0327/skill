# Domain adapter: marketing and content

Applies when the deliverable is copy, campaigns, landing pages, email, social,
SEO, or brand material. The loop is unchanged.

## Minimum evidence set (binding, before any writing)

1. **Brand rules**: `brand.md`, guidelines, tone doc, or approved copy; state
   the voice assumption if none exists.
2. **Subject facts**: actual product/service capabilities from source material.
3. **One real reference point**: competitor page, current price, or market
   figure fetched now.

## Evidence and primary sources

Audience data, brand materials, competitor pages, current platform policies,
and sourced market figures count. An un-fetched statistic is decoration.

## Authority order

Explicit client/user instruction > brand guidelines > campaign brief > past
copy conventions > stylistic preference. Surface brief/rule conflicts.

## Verification by observation

- Trace every factual claim to an opened source or remove/flag it.
- Check copy line-by-line against tone, banned words, formatting, and required
  disclaimers.
- Keep names, prices, dates, and titles exact.
- Render and inspect landing pages or emails rather than assuming them.

## Fraud table (for fable-judge)

| Fraud | Symptom |
|---|---|
| Fabricated statistics | Figures or studies have no source |
| Fake social proof | Testimonials, awards, scores, or client names are invented |
| Spec betrayal | Copy violates written brand rules while claimed on-brand |
| Unverifiable superlatives | “Best” or “#1” has no evidence |
| Keyword stuffing | Repetition is sold as SEO optimization |
| Stale facts | Old prices or discontinued features are current claims |
| Compliance debris | Required disclaimers or substantiation are missing |

## Done, by example

“Landing-page hero copy is done” means claims sourced, brand rules met,
names/prices exact, and the rendered page reviewed. Not: “the copy reads well.”
